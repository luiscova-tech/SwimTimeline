# Time Standards Sources

Benchmarks in `../extracted/mila-timeline.md`, `../extracted/mila-events.json`, and `../extracted/mila-daily-events.json` use:

- USA Swimming 2024-2028 Motivational Standards (two-year age group), 11-12 Girls LCM: https://www.usaswimming.org/times/popular-resources/motivational-times
- AZSI 2025-2026 Age Group State Qualifying Time Standards, Women 11-12 LCM: https://www.azswimming.org/azswimming/UserFiles/File/azsi-age-group-state-qualifying-time-standards-2025-2026_080831.pdf
- AZSI 2025-2026 Age Group Regional Qualifying Time Standards, Women 11-12 LCM: https://www.azswimming.org/azswimming/UserFiles/File/azsi-age-group-regional-qualifying-time-standards-2025-2026_068948.pdf

Standards were matched by swimmer age, gender, course, event, and verified seed time.
